— ABOUT US

About Balaji Earthmovers

Established in 1991, Balaji Earthmovers is an experienced excavation and earthworks contractor serving residential, commercial and infrastructure projects across Mumbai and the Mumbai Metropolitan Region.

With extensive expertise in deep basement excavation, hydraulic rock breaking, soil and muck removal, site development and infrastructure earthworks, we provide integrated groundwork solutions tailored to the demands of modern construction.

Our strength lies in our in-house machinery, experienced operators and coordinated execution capabilities. By bringing excavation, rock breaking and material haulage together under one operational framework, we help clients streamline site operations and manage complex groundwork requirements efficiently.

At Balaji Earthmovers, we believe that every successful structure begins with a well-prepared foundation. We are committed to dependable execution, professional coordination and long-term relationships built on trust.

— CAPABILITIES

Our Core Capabilities

Integrated groundwork solutions delivered with our in-house machinery and experienced site teams across Mumbai and the MMR region.

01

Deep Basement Excavation

Execution of deep basement excavation for residential, commercial and mixed-use developments, including bulk excavation, controlled cutting and trimming to the required formation level, as per approved drawings and site requirements.

02

Hydraulic Rock Breaking

Mechanical rock breaking using hydraulic breaker attachments for hard-rock excavation in urban and built-up locations, subject to applicable site conditions and permissions.

03

Soil and Muck Removal

Integrated loading, transportation and authorized disposal of excavated soil and debris, coordinated with excavation activities to support efficient site operations.

04

Site Development and Levelling

Site grading, earth cutting, filling, levelling and related groundwork to prepare plots for subsequent construction activities.

05

Infrastructure and Metro Earthworks

Earthwork execution for infrastructure projects, including large-scale excavation and associated groundwork in coordination with EPC contractors and project teams.

06

Demolition and Structural Breaking

Mechanical breaking and removal of concrete slabs, foundations and other structures to facilitate redevelopment, new excavation and site preparation.

— BEFORE YOU CALL

The questions we get asked.

Straight answers on scope, scale and paperwork. If yours is not here, the desk answers the phone Monday to Saturday.

ASK SOMETHING ELSE →
1. Which locations do you serve?

We are based in Dahisar East, Mumbai, and undertake excavation and earthworks projects across Mumbai and the Mumbai Metropolitan Region, subject to project requirements and operational feasibility.

2. What types of excavation do you undertake?

Our services include deep basement excavation, hard-rock breaking, soil and muck removal, site levelling, demolition-related breaking and infrastructure earthworks.

3. Can you undertake rock excavation without blasting?

We provide mechanical rock breaking using hydraulic breaker attachments. The method adopted depends on geological conditions, site constraints and applicable permissions.

4. Do you provide soil transportation along with excavation?

Yes. We offer coordinated excavation, loading and soil transportation services. Disposal arrangements are assessed according to the material, destination and applicable requirements.

5. Is your machinery owned or hired?

Our fleet includes in-house machinery and support equipment. Please contact our team to confirm equipment availability for your specific project.

6. Do you work with developers and EPC contractors?

Yes. We undertake work for property developers, contractors, infrastructure companies and institutional clients, subject to project scope and requirements.

7. What information is required for a quotation?

Please share the project location, plot details, proposed excavation depth, number of basement levels, estimated excavation quantity, soil and rock conditions, access constraints and expected start date. Drawings and BOQs are helpful where available.

8. How can I arrange a site visit?

Call or WhatsApp our team on +91 93203 20445 or email balajiearthmovers5268@gmail.com to discuss your project and arrange a site assessment.
