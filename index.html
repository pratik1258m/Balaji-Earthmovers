— BEFORE YOU CALL

The questions we get asked.

Straight answers on scope, scale and paperwork. If yours is not here, the desk answers the phone Monday to Saturday.

ASK SOMETHING ELSE →
Which parts of Mumbai do you work in?

We are based at Dahisar (East), right by the Western Express Highway, and work across Mumbai and MMR. Most of our listed works are in the Western and Central suburbs — Malad, Goregaon, Borivali, Kandivali, Vile Parle, Santacruz, Andheri, Powai, Kanjurmarg, Ghatkopar and Lalbagh.

What size excavation do you take on?

Our listed works run from 15,000 cu.m over one month up to 4,00,000 cu.m over twelve. That is the practical band — smaller jobs are worth discussing too, but the fleet is really built for multi-level basement and infrastructure scale.

Can you get through hard rock without blasting?

Yes. We hold 24 hydraulic rock breaker attachments matched to our own excavators, so basalt is broken mechanically. This is what makes rock sites workable in built-up areas where blasting permission is not realistically available. On Northern Heights alone, 1,50,000 cu.m of that rock came out this way.

Do you handle soil removal and disposal as well as digging?

Yes — 26 Hyva tippers and 3 Tata dumpers run loading and haulage under the same contract, so excavation and removal are not fighting each other for priority. Mention your preferred disposal location in the enquiry and we will price haulage to it.

Is the machinery owned by you, or hired in?

Owned and operated by us. All 81 units — excavators, breakers, tippers, dumpers, backhoe loaders and pickups — are our own plant with our own operators and drivers. That is the main reason a schedule slip can be recovered instead of re-quoted.

Can you put many machines on one site at once?

Yes. With 24 excavators and 29 loading and haulage units we routinely run combined fleets, and we are currently executing eight ongoing sites in parallel. If your programme needs a specific number of machines on plot, tell us the count and the dates and we will confirm availability.

Do you work with developers, EPC contractors or both?

Both. Our client list includes property developers (Omkar Realty, N Rose, Pashmina, Chandak, Veena, Bhoomi, Arked, V.G.S), infrastructure and EPC contractors (L&T ECC Division, J. Kumar Infra, Relcon Infraproject) and institutions such as SVKM.

What do you need from me to give an estimate?

Site location, the plot area and depth or number of basement levels, the approximate volume if your structural consultant has one, expected soil and rock conditions, site access and any restricted working hours, and your target start date. The estimate desk form on this page asks for exactly these.

Are you GST registered, and will we get a proper invoice?

Yes. We work as a registered firm — Builders & Contractors, established 1991 — and raise proper tax invoices against your own GSTIN, so your accounts and input-credit side gets what it needs. Our office will share our registration details directly at the contracting stage.
